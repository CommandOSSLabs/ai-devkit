# ADR Template (Portable Baseline)

# NNNN — [short title]

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play? What problem or constraint prompts a decision? State
the read/write pattern, the failure mode, or the ambiguity that two reasonable
people could resolve differently.

## Decision

The choice we made, in one or two sentences. Active voice: "We do X."

## Alternatives Considered (Optional)

- [Option]: benefits, drawbacks, and rejection reason. Include only when the
  alternatives carry real trade-offs worth preserving.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we're now committed to, and what
we explicitly chose _not_ to do.
